# Commercial use

Castellum is free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

That covers, for example:

- personal use, including hobby projects and private study;
- charities, schools, public research bodies, public health and safety
  organisations, environmental bodies and government institutions, as the
  licence defines them.

Keep the attribution: anyone you pass Castellum on to must get the licence
and the notice "Required Notice: Copyright 2026 Riera Technologies Ltd"
(see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).

## When you need a paid licence

Any other use needs a commercial licence from Riera Technologies Ltd. That
includes using Castellum in or for a business, selling it, bundling it with a
product or service, or building a commercial product from its code.

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository titled "Commercial licence request". Say who
you are, roughly how you want to use Castellum and for how many people. Do not
put confidential details in the issue; we will move the conversation to a
private channel.
